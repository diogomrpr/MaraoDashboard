## Why
Marao is named for Serra do Marão. Its landscape palette uses mist, schist, muted slate blue, forest green, heather, ochre and earth to connect the dashboard to that origin. A broader set of photographs across spring, summer, autumn and winter will refine these colors and supply distinct accents that the initial palette lacks.

## What Changes
- Apply the approved light and dark surfaces and primary pairings to the shared theme.
- Carry the palette into slider colors, weather treatments and chart series while retaining semantic state distinctions.
- Recolor existing mountain artwork and regenerate native/documentation exports without changing its geometry.
- Align theme and brand documentation, with reference-photo attribution.
- Refine the eight existing domain colors using 15 real Marão photo references, keeping a cleaner forest green and separating yellow from orange.
- Expand the shared chart palette from six to ten mode-aware colors, adding river teal, winter blue, rose and lilac while retaining the mineral base and current brand artwork.
- Document source and capture-date confidence, raw extracted swatches and the separate contrast-adjusted UI palette.

## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- `mobile-theme-layout`: Marão landscape colors and readable control pairings.

## Impact
Shared theme, chart renderer, slider fallbacks, SVG artwork/exports and documentation. The seasonal refinement changes theme/chart accents and documentation only; it preserves the mineral surfaces, slider palette and existing brand exports. No saved dashboard, entity, configuration-version or geometry changes.
